# Role Play catalog sections

My characters, system characters and community show All, Trending, Favorites and Group Rooms below the existing search/filter row. Trending sorts existing trending_score, falling back to usage_count. Favorites are account-specific and stored on the server; preset copies share their preset key, custom characters use their own ID.

Group Rooms shows the user's multi-character rooms in personal/system catalogs and public room templates in Community. Public rooms require every member character to be public. Publishing shares the character group, not messages, documents or memories. Starting someone else's public room creates a private conversation with cloned public characters and reuses it on later visits until deleted. Unpublishing any member makes the template unavailable.

Creation includes Custom, From a story and Create a chat room. The room picker defaults to the user's collection; the existing system-character option is retained. Visibility can be selected during creation or changed from a group-room card.
